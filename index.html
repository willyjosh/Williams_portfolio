About Me

The Artist

My name is Williams Joshua, a Nigerian visual artist and Mechatronics Engineering student with a passion for creativity, technology and storytelling.

My work includes pencil drawing, oil painting, murals and custom artworks. I enjoy taking ordinary ideas and transforming them into visual pieces that carry emotion and meaning.

I am also interested in the relationship between art and technology and hope to explore new ways of combining both worlds.
