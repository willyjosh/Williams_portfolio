About the artist

I'm Williams.
I make things that last.

I am an artist and creative based in Nigeria, working across pencil, oil painting, murals and experimental visual art.

My approach is simple: pay attention, tell the truth of the subject and make every mark intentional.

I am especially interested in the meeting point between traditional art, technology and new ways of telling stories.

The process

From idea to art.

A simple process built around communication, detail and intention.

01

Discover

We discuss your idea, reference, story, space and the feeling you want the finished piece to carry.

02

Develop

I study references, compositions and materials before turning the strongest direction into the artwork.

03

Create

The artwork comes together through drawing, painting, layering, correction and detail.

04

Deliver

The finished piece is reviewed, prepared carefully and delivered according to the project.
